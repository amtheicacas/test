# Items the attorney must confirm or approve

A running list for the Association's attorney. Nothing here is legal advice from the app's developer: the statute text
could not be fetched during development, so every reference to FS 720.305 below is from memory and UNVERIFIED.
Add to this list; do not delete (mark answered items with the date and answer instead).

## A. Decision letter and findings (added after the user's question)
1. Does FS 720.305, or our Declaration or the Fining Resolution (Apr 17, 2023, Exhibits 1-3), require written findings
   or a stated reason in the committee's decision notice? (Today the committee types "Findings" on the meeting page
   and it prints in the decision letter; the box is required.)
2. If not required: is the attorney comfortable with standard pre-filled findings text that the committee can edit
   (for example: "The committee reviewed the evidence presented and found the violation occurred and was not
   corrected by the deadline.")? If yes, please supply the approved wording.

## B. Letter wording (Part B; letters stay frozen until the final Word files arrive)
3. Final wording of the new Notice of Violation, Notice of Hearing and decision letter (all letters are under legal review).
4. Notice of Hearing: photo(s) at the bottom, an "at a glance" box, and mention of the My Heron Landing Application and
   the self-help right (user said yes to all three; attorney to approve the wording).
5. Decision letter: removal of the "No fine if cured by" field and its bullet (user's decision; please confirm).
6. Removal of every mention of suspension from the letters (no amenities; suspension is in the Declaration but not

8. The Notice of Hearing attaches the saved ORIGINAL Notice of Violation only (user's decision): confirm that is enough.

## C. Fines and process
10. Confirm the fine rules as built: $100/violation; continuing violation $100/day up to $1,000 aggregate; at least 14
    days' notice (the app warns under 20); hearing before a committee of at least 3 members who are not officers,
    directors or employees; majority approval; fine due 30 days after approval (+5 days mail buffer).
11. How per-day continuing fines should be counted.
12. Certified mail with return receipt for the hearing notice (and Letter 3): confirm what must be kept as proof.

## D. Records
13. Retention: the Association has decided 7 years. Confirm, and say what must be kept that long (letters, photos,
    committee site-visit notes, email copies, backups).
14. Committee site-visit observations are internal (committee, Board and manager only; never owners). Confirm they may be
    kept that way, and whether they would have to be produced in a dispute.
15. Official-records and privacy questions about the owner and email data held by the app.

## E. Added after the certified-mail tracking feature (Notice of Hearing)
16. Notice of Hearing goes by email, certified mail (return receipt) AND first-class mail. If the certified letter comes back
    unclaimed, refused or undeliverable, is the first-class copy (and the email) enough notice, or must the Association
    do something more (re-send, post at the property, hand deliver) or move the hearing? The application only WARNS the
    Board and manager; it does not change the case, block the hearing or send anything by itself.
17. How long should the Association wait for a delivery result before treating a certified letter as not delivered?
    (The application reminds staff to check USPS tracking after 7 days; that number is only a starting point.)
18. What must be kept as proof of mailing and delivery for each letter: the certified receipt (PS Form 3800), the signed
    return receipt (green card or electronic), the USPS tracking history, the first-class mailing record? The application
    stores the mailing date, tracking number and result for each letter and includes them in the case file.

## F. Tenants (added 2026-11-21)
19. To save postage, the Association mails (first class or certified) ONLY the owner and does not mail a separate copy to a tenant; a tenant with an email on file is emailed a copy. Does FS 720.305, the Declaration or the Fining Resolution require the notice of violation, the notice of hearing or the decision letter to be delivered to the tenant (occupant) as well as the owner, and by what method (mail, email, posting)? Fines are charged to the owner's account; if a tenant rather than the owner is the person "sought to be fined", who must receive the notice? The Fining Resolution exhibits say a notice is "also being provided to the record Owner(s)" when the lot is occupied by someone else: should the tenant version of the letters still be used?

## G. Added after the second stabilisation pass (2026-11-21)
20. Conflict of interest: when a Board member or the manager is also the OWNER of the property named in a violation (one login for both), may that person dismiss the case, accept it as cured, record the Board's vote or approve the imposition of the fine on their own household's case? Today the app lets them (a committee member is blocked by the abstention rule; a Board member or manager is not). We recommend another Board member or the manager must act. Do the Declaration, the Fining Resolution or FS 720.305 say anything about it?
21. A fine was imposed and posted to the owner's ledger, and then the violation is corrected, or the Board dismisses or waives the case, or the matter has gone to the attorney: what must happen to the posted charge (stay, reduce, reverse), what must the owner be told, and can a payment still be recorded once the case is with the attorney? Today the app closes the case ("corrected, no fine" or "dismissed") without a warning that the ledger charge and the unpaid fine still stand.
22. The decision letter and the extension letter print "Copy also sent to the tenant/owner" whenever a tenant is on record. To save postage only the owner gets a paper copy, and a tenant without an email address gets no copy at all (see question 19). Should that sentence be changed to say only what was actually sent, or must a copy always go to the tenant?
23. If one letter of a Notice of Hearing fails at the print service (for example one of the addresses is refused) while the other letters go out, is the notice still validly given, what must the Association do and how soon, and must the hearing be moved? (Related to question 16, which is about letters that were mailed but not delivered.) Today the app marks the notice mailed and shows the failed address only in a staff note on the case.
