# Statuses and labels, for review

Change any wording you like and send it back. "Owner sees" is the label owners see in their portal; blank means the same as the staff label.

## 1. Case statuses (a violation is always in exactly one)
| # | Staff label (today) | Owner sees | Counts as open? | When it happens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Draft | (hidden from owners) | open | Saved but not sent |
| 2 | Cure period running | **In Cure Period** (yellow) | open | Notice sent; the owner has time to fix it |
| 3 | Awaiting board vote | **Sent to Board for Fine** (orange) | open | Cure time ended without a cure (or referred early / point in time) |
| 4 | Board approved — schedule hearing | | open | Board voted to issue a fine; waiting to be put on a committee meeting |
| 5 | Sent to Compliance Committee | (red) | open | Assigned to a committee meeting; hearing notice sent |
| 6 | Committee decided: awaiting Board review | | open | Committee submitted its meeting results; Board must approve |
| 7 | Imposed by committee — awaiting payment / cure | | open (owners see it as **Closed**) | Board approved a fine; letter sent; waiting for payment |
| 8 | Rejected by committee | | ended (**Closed** for owners) | Committee did not approve the fine |
| 9 | Waived by the Committee (closed) | | ended (**Closed**) | Committee waived it (for example already fixed) |
| 10 | Cured | | ended (**Closed**) | Marked corrected, no fine |
| 11 | Closed (cured and paid) | | ended (**Closed**) | Fine paid and violation corrected |
| 12 | Dismissed | | ended (**Closed**) | Board or manager dismissed it |
| 13 | On hold | | open | Paused until a date, then the cure period restarts |
| 14 | Referred to attorney | | **ended in the code** (see note) | Sent to the attorney |

**Closed for owners** = cured, closed, dismissed, rejected, waived, and fine imposed. Everything else shows as open.

## 2. Labels that replace the status in certain situations
| Situation | Label shown |
|---|---|
| Owner says it's fixed and staff marked it for re-inspection | Re-inspect |
| A hold ended and the cure period restarted | Off hold: cure period restarted (staff) |

## 3. Progress tracker (the six boxes)
Notice issued · Cure period · Fine issued by Board · Violation sent to Compliance Committee / Homeowner Hearing · Fine Imposed & Posted to Ledger · Closed
Special boxes: **Referred to attorney** (added after Fine Imposed), **Bypassed** (a step the case skipped).

## 4. Flags and badges (labels, not statuses)
Point in time (purple) · Safety issue · Board declared emergency · Re-inspect · Mail held until [date] · Mailing cancelled: corrected first, postage saved · Direct referral / Normal path (legal) · Unread message (envelope)

## 5. Committee decision choices
Fine confirmed (as proposed) · Fine confirmed, amount changed · Waived / resolved: close the violation · Fine rejected

## 6. Committee meeting results status
Committee working · Awaiting Board review · Board reviewed

## 7. Letters ("notice kinds")
Notice of violation · Second notice of violation · Extension granted · Notice of committee hearing · Notice of committee decision (written findings)
Delivery: First-class mail · Certified, return receipt · Hand delivered

## 8. Issuance timing
Standard: email now, mail after the delay · Immediate: urgent, email and mail now · Draft

## Things worth deciding
1. **Referred to attorney** is treated as "ended" by the code. That means owners can't send messages on it and it drops out of open counts. It probably should count as open.
2. An imposed fine shows as **Closed** for owners, as you asked, but the owner may still owe it. The home tile shows "Fine due [date]" so they don't miss it.
3. "Cure period running" is the staff wording; owners see "In Cure Period". Do you want one wording for both?
4. Statuses 3 and 4 both read like "waiting on the Board". Board vote vs. scheduling a hearing may be clearer with different names.
